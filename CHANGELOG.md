# Changelog

- 2026-10-23, 1.1.0: Alt line grid in the player sheet. One distribution per prop (Poisson or negative binomial for REB, AST, 3PM; normal or log-normal for PTS and PRA) fitted to the no vig main line, then every rung priced with fair odds, best book price and EV, with rungs at or above your EV bar flagged. Added a Grid over cell to the line slider. New opt-in Alternate lines setting (off by default, doubles credits per game).
- 1.0.0: First release.
